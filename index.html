Take the photo. Skip the studio.

Photograph what you’re selling, wherever it is. ShelfReady lifts it out and sets it on a clean backdrop with a soft shadow, ready to list. It all happens on your iPhone, and nothing is uploaded.

Free to download. Your first 3 saves are free, in full quality.

Fifteen backgrounds to try it on.

Five studio backgrounds and ten colors. In the app you see your own photo on every one before you pick. Here are the same headphones on all fifteen.

Four steps, all on the phone.

No account to make, nothing to upload, no computer needed.

  1. Step 1

    Shoot it, or pick it

    Take a photo with the camera in the app, or pick up to 10 from your library. One item on a plain surface, in good light, works best.

  2. Step 2

    The background comes off

    ShelfReady finds the item and lifts it off the couch, the floor or the bed. Drag across the result to see it next to your photo.

  3. Step 3

    Choose a background

    Five studio backgrounds and ten colors. In a batch, one background goes on the lot and any single photo can be switched on its own.

  4. Step 4

    Save it, sized for your listing

    Every save comes out in the shape and size your marketplace asks for. The gallery keeps it, with the original, for next time.

Tell it what you sell.

Clothing, sneakers, bags, watches, electronics or beauty. Each one opens on a background that suits it, and you can switch whenever you like. The small picture is the photo it started from.

  • Clothing opens on White.

  • Sneakers open on Light grey.

  • Bags open on Light grey.

  • Watches open on Charcoal.

  • Electronics opens on Contrast.

  • Beauty opens on Warm.

The finer controls.

For when the first try isn’t quite it, and for the tenth item of the day.

Fix the cutout
If it picks the wrong thing, draw a box around the item you want to keep and it cuts again from inside the box.
Keep the real setting
Sometimes the room is part of the sale. Keep the original background and just fix the light.
Fine-tune
Adjust the shadow, the background warmth, the brightness and the contrast on any result.
Up to ten at once
Pick up to 10 photos and watch them come through one by one. With a subscription, save the whole batch in one go.
Sized for where you sell
Presets for seven popular marketplaces: square, 4:3 or portrait 3:4, as a JPEG. Or keep your own shape at full size, in JPEG or HEIC.
Save or share
Save to Photos, or share straight to another app.
A gallery with the originals
Finished photos stay in the app next to the photo they came from, so you can try another background later without shooting the item again. Settings shows the space they use and clears it.

Your photos stay on your phone.

Finding the item, removing the background and balancing the light all happen on your iPhone, using Apple’s on-device frameworks. Your photos are never uploaded, and there is no account to make.

The rest, plainly: the free version shows ads, and the app uses analytics and crash reports, with your consent where the law asks for it. Where consent is asked, you can change your answer in the app’s Settings. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is collected and why.

Try it on your own things first.

Process as many photos as you like and try every background. A subscription is only needed to keep saving after the first three, or to save a whole batch at once.
