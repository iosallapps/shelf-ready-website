2. What ShelfReady does

ShelfReady turns a photo of something you sell into a listing-ready product photo: it lifts the item off whatever it was lying on, sets it on a clean backdrop with a soft shadow, and sizes the result for the marketplace you choose. All of this runs on your iPhone.
